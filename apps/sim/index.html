References (R35)
All evidence, standards and technical references cited in the TaskLink SIH 2026 submission.
  • 1
    Bharat Taxi / Sahakar Taxi Scheme — NAFSC
    National Agricultural and Federation Services Cooperative (NAFSC), Ministry of Cooperation, GoI. 6.37 lakh drivers operating at zero commission under the Sahakar Taxi initiative as of 2024. URL: https://nafscl.in/sahakar-taxi
  • 2
    India's Booming Gig and Platform Economy — NITI Aayog
    NITI Aayog, Government of India (2022). "India's Booming Gig and Platform Economy: Perspectives and Recommendations on the Future of Work." Estimates 23.5 million platform workers by 2029-30. URL: https://niti.gov.in/reports
  • 3
    NSS 79th Round — Household Social Consumption on ICT (MoSPI CAMS)
    Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), NSS 79th Round (2022-23). Table on mobile handset type by rural/urban. 9.7% urban and 12.3% rural mobile users on keypad-only devices. URL: https://mospi.gov.in/nss-79th-round
  • 4
    CMS India Telecom Subscription Reports 2025 — TRAI
    Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) / CMS. Quarterly telecom subscription data showing feature-phone (non-VoLTE 4G) subscriber breakdown by state. URL: https://trai.gov.in/release-publication/reports/telecom-subscriptions-reports
  • 5
    Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act 2002
    Government of India. The Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act 2002 and 2023 Amendment. Provides the legal framework for cooperative federation governance, member voting rights, and democratic management. URL: https://mscs.dac.gov.in
  • 6
    National Cooperative Database — Ministry of Cooperation
    Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India. National Cooperative Database tracking registered cooperative societies, federations, and membership across all states. URL: https://ncd.nddb.coop
  • 7
    e-Shram Portal — Ministry of Labour and Employment
    Ministry of Labour and Employment, GoI. e-Shram Portal for registration of unorganized workers with UAN (Universal Account Number). Basis for PMSBY and PMJJBY insurance coverage eligibility. URL: https://eshram.gov.in
  • 8
    Bhashini — AI4Bharat / MeitY
    Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) / AI4Bharat. Bhashini Digital India Language Platform providing ASR, NMT (translation), and TTS in all 22 scheduled Indian languages. API docs: https://bhashini.gov.in/developer
  • 9
    NPCI UPI and *99# USSD Specifications
    National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). Unified Payments Interface (UPI) protocol and *99# USSD banking specification enabling no-internet payment capability. URL: https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/99/product-overview
  • 10
    DIGIPIN — India Post / MapmyIndia (Mappls)
    India Post and Survey of India (with MapmyIndia as technical partner). DIGIPIN: Open 10-character digital addressing system dividing India into a 4m×4m grid. Open-source algorithm: https://github.com/IndiaPost/digipin
  • 11
    Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act)
    Government of India. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Sections 11 (data access), 12 (right to erasure), 8(9) (purpose limitation and data minimisation). URL: https://meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf
  • 12
    PMSBY — Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana
    Ministry of Finance, GoI. Accidental death and disability insurance at ₹20/year for e-Shram registered workers aged 18-70. Coverage: ₹2 lakh. URL: https://jansuraksha.gov.in/pmsby
  • 13
    PMJJBY — Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana
    Ministry of Finance, GoI. Life insurance at ₹436/year for e-Shram registered workers aged 18-50. Coverage: ₹2 lakh. URL: https://jansuraksha.gov.in/pmjjby
  • 14
    Exotel — Cloud Telephony Platform
    Exotel Techcom Pvt. Ltd. Cloud telephony APIs for bidirectional audio streaming, DTMF IVR, and outbound call campaigns. Used for keypad worker voice agent. Docs: https://developer.exotel.com
  • 15
    Noto Fonts — Google
    Google Noto Font family providing complete Unicode coverage for all 22 scheduled Indian languages including Noto Sans Ol Chiki (Santali), Noto Sans Meetei Mayek (Manipuri), Noto Nastaliq Urdu. URL: https://fonts.google.com/noto
